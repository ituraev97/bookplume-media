## Decision
WHAT HAPPENS — "What Happens to Your Body Every Second You Hold Your Breath" (title formula from PIPELINE performance rules: time unit + everyday moment).
- Interactive hook borrowed from the breath-hold outlier: "take a breath and hold it", an on-screen YOUR HOLD timer runs for chapters 0–3 (turns red "breathe any time" after 60 s; safety line "sit down, dry land, breathe whenever you need").
- Surprise: at the breaking point blood O2 is usually still ≥ 98% — the urge is CO2/pH (central chemoreceptors). Not used in the last 4 long videos (swallow, leg muscles, stand up/baroreflex, skull).
