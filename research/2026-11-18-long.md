## e. Facts (checked) and sources
- Cardiovascular centers in the medulla (cardioaccelerator, cardioinhibitor, vasomotor); carotid sinuses at the base of the internal carotid arteries; aortic sinuses; low-pressure baroreceptors in the venae cavae + right atrium; falling BP → baroreceptor firing ↓ → sympathetic ↑ to heart and vessels; endocrine regulation (renin–angiotensin, ADH) — OpenStax A&P 2e 20.4: https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology/pages/20-4-homeostatic-regulation-of-the-vascular-system
- Systemic veins hold ~64% of blood volume; high capacitance; valves; gravity → pooling in the lower limbs — OpenStax 20.1: https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology/pages/20-1-structure-and-function-of-blood-vessels
- Skeletal muscle pump + respiratory pump; recruits flex legs at attention, pooling → less oxygenated blood to the brain → may lose consciousness — OpenStax 20.2: https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology/pages/20-2-blood-flow-blood-pressure-and-resistance
- 300–800 mL pools in the lower extremities; ↓ venous return → ↓ cardiac output (Frank–Starling); baroreceptor reflex = ↓ vagal tone + ↑ sympathetic tone constricting arterioles and splanchnic veins; MAP = CO × SVR; healthy BP returns within 15 s; OH = ≥ 20 systolic or ≥ 10 diastolic mmHg within 3 min — StatPearls, Orthostatic Hypotension (read via statpearls.com mirror): https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK448192/
- Arterial baroreceptors in the carotid sinus + aortic arch; carotid sinus → glossopharyngeal (carotid sinus nerve), aortic arch → vagus; NTS in the medulla; beat-to-beat; ↑ sympathetic, HR, contractility, resistance — StatPearls, Physiology, Baroreceptors (statpearls.com mirror): https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK538172/
- Initial BP drop bottoms out ≈ 10 s; baroreflex peaks ≈ 10 s; cerebral autoregulation buffers within 10 s — Harms et al. 2021, Auton Neurosci 231:102756: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.autneu.2020.102756
- HR rises then decreases toward baseline; skeletal muscle contraction + baroreflex drive it — Quinn et al. 2022, Curr Aging Sci: https://doi.org/10.2174/1874609815666220419102648
- Symptoms (dizziness, blurred vision, fainting), autonomic NS makes heart beat faster + vessels narrow — MedlinePlus Genetics: https://medlineplus.gov/genetics/condition/orthostatic-hypotension
- Common carotid divides at the level of the upper border of the thyroid cartilage — Gray's Anatomy 1918, p. 550 (text beside Fig. 507).
- Plates: Gray's Anatomy 1918 Figs. 505, 507, 581, 591, 682, 793 (rights.json).
