## c. Decision
**Skull Anatomy Quiz: Can You Score 27/27?** Friday = QUIZ. The skull was the producer's #11 long-quiz suggestion (performance rules 2026-11-16); our IG skull posts (orbit bones, facial bones) save well; Gray's 1918 has 5 excellent coloured skull plates (side, front, sagittal, base, infratemporal). ## e. Facts (checked) and sources
- 22 skull bones: 8 cranial (frontal, 2 parietal, 2 temporal, occipital, sphenoid, ethmoid) + 14 facial; mandible the only movable bone; coronal/squamous/lambdoid/sagittal sutures; pterion; nasal conchae (inferior = separate bone, superior + middle = ethmoid); vomer + perpendicular plate of ethmoid form the septum; mental + infraorbital foramina; sella turcica + hypophyseal fossa holding the pituitary; cribriform plate + olfactory foramina; foramen magnum — OpenStax A&P 2e 7.2: https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology/pages/7-2-the-skull
- Foramen rotundum = maxillary nerve (V2), ovale = mandibular nerve (V3) (+ accessory meningeal a.), spinosum = middle meningeal artery; cribriform plate = olfactory nerve; optic canal etc. — TeachMeAnatomy, Cranial Foramina: https://teachmeanatomy.info/head/osteology/cranial-foramina/
- Middle meningeal artery through foramen spinosum, runs deep to the pterion — TeachMeAnatomy: https://teachmeanatomy.info/encyclopaedia/m/middle-meningeal-artery/ ; Radiopaedia, Pterion: https://radiopaedia.org/articles/pterion
- Temporal bone: petrous part, styloid process (styloglossus, stylohyoid, stylopharyngeus), external acoustic meatus — TeachMeAnatomy: https://teachmeanatomy.info/head/osteology/temporal-bone/ ; Gray 1918 p. 184 (text beside Fig. 189).
- Plates: Gray's Anatomy 1918 Figs. 188, 189, 190, 193, 194 (rights.json); scan https://archive.org/details/anatomyofhumanbo00grayrich
