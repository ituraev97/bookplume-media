What you'll remember:
• Rectus femoris is the only quadriceps muscle that crosses both the hip and the knee.
• Sartorius is the longest muscle in the body (ASIS → medial tibia).
• Piriformis is the landmark of the buttock: superior gluteal nerve above it, sciatic nerve below.
• Femoral nerve = front of the thigh, obturator nerve = inner thigh, tibial nerve = back of the leg, deep fibular = front of the leg, superficial fibular = lateral leg.
• Exam trap: the short head of biceps femoris is supplied by the common fibular part of the sciatic nerve; the long head by the tibial part.
