What you'll remember:
• The skull has 22 bones: 8 cranial + 14 facial. The mandible is the only one that moves.
• Coronal suture = frontal ↔ parietals; squamous = temporal ↔ parietal; lambdoid = occipital ↔ parietals (shaped like the Greek λ).
• Pterion = where frontal, parietal, temporal and sphenoid meet; the middle meningeal artery runs just under it.
• "Standing Room Only": superior orbital fissure (V1), foramen rotundum (V2), foramen ovale (V3). Foramen spinosum = middle meningeal artery.
• Cribriform plate = olfactory nerve (CN I); hypophyseal fossa of the sella turcica = pituitary gland; foramen magnum = medulla oblongata → spinal cord.
