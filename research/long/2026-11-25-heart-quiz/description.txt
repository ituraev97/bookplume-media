What you'll remember:
• The right ventricle forms most of the front of the heart and pumps to the lungs through the pulmonary artery; the pulmonary veins return oxygen-rich blood to the left atrium.
• Valves: tricuspid (3 cusps) between right atrium and ventricle, mitral/bicuspid (2 cusps) on the left; aortic and pulmonary valves have 3 semilunar cusps and their closing makes the second heart sound.
• Right coronary artery = right atrium + right ventricle; left anterior descending = front of the left ventricle, the apex and the front two thirds of the septum.
• Inside the right atrium: crista terminalis (SA node sits near its upper end), pectinate muscles ("comb"), fossa ovalis (remnant of the foramen ovale).
• The aortic arch gives 3 branches: brachiocephalic trunk, left common carotid, left subclavian. The bundle of His carries the signal from the AV node into the interventricular septum.
