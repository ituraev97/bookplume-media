# Sources — What happens to a pill you swallow? (JOURNEY 3D, 2026-11-19)
- Merck Manual (consumer), Drug Administration — "most drugs are absorbed from the small intestine"; "The drug passes through the intestinal wall and travels to the liver before being transported via the bloodstream"; "The intestinal wall and liver chemically alter (metabolize) many drugs" https://www.merckmanuals.com/home/drugs/administration-and-kinetics-of-drugs/drug-administration
- Merck Manual, Drug Absorption — enteric coatings delay dissolution until the small intestine; disintegration depends on additives/compression (→ "most tablets") https://www.merckmanuals.com/home/drugs/administration-and-kinetics-of-drugs/drug-absorption
- Merck Manual, Drug Elimination — "Most drugs, particularly water-soluble drugs and their metabolites, are eliminated by the kidneys through urine." https://www.merckmanuals.com/home/drugs/administration-and-kinetics-of-drugs/drug-elimination
- OpenStax A&P 2e 23.4 The Stomach — "HCl is responsible for the high acidity (pH 1.5 to 3.5) of the stomach contents" https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/23-4-the-stomach
- OpenStax A&P 2e 20.5 Circulatory Pathways — hepatic portal vein delivers materials directly to the liver; liver drains via hepatic veins into the IVC https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/20-5-circulatory-pathways
- NIDDK Your Kidneys & How They Work — "filter about 150 quarts of blood"; "about a million filtering units called nephrons" https://www.niddk.nih.gov/health-information/kidney-disease/kidneys-how-they-work
- StatPearls, First-Pass Effect — liver + GI tract sites of first-pass metabolism https://www.statpearls.com/articlelibrary/viewarticle/21783/
Fact-check (independent agent): 4 fixes applied — "In acid this strong, the tablet starts to break apart" → "In stomach acid, most tablets start to break apart and dissolve"; "The liver can break down…" → "Your gut wall and liver can break down…"; "150 quarts from your blood" → "150 quarts of blood" (NIDDK wording); BLOODSTREAM chip "carried all over your body" → "via the heart → your whole body" (liver → hepatic veins → IVC → heart → aorta).
