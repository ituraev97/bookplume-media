# Liver quiz (SHEET QUIZ) — 2026-11-23
Plate: Gray's Anatomy 1918, Fig. 1086 "Inferior surface of the liver" (Internet Archive anatomyofhumanbo00grayrich, public domain). All 5 answers use Gray's own printed names/labels.
Facts (opened sources):
- Gallbladder "stores and concentrates bile", capacity ~50 mL — Britannica https://www.britannica.com/science/gallbladder
- Right lobe is the largest of the four lobes (right, left, caudate, quadrate) — Kenhub https://kenhub.com/en/library/anatomy/liver ; StatPearls (statpearls.com mirror) https://www.statpearls.com/ArticleLibrary/viewarticle/24362
- Left lobe: Gray's 1918 labels a "Gastric impression" on its underside (Fig. 1086); Cunningham's Textbook: inferior surface of the left lobe "rests upon the stomach" (search snippet, alamy page of the book text). Spoken line softened to "Its underside touches your stomach."
- Inferior vena cava "carries deoxygenated blood from the lower half of the body to the right atrium" — Wikipedia https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inferior_vena_cava ; hepatic veins drain into the IVC, IVC fossa on the liver — Kenhub liver page
- Caudate lobe sits between the fissure for the ligamentum venosum and the IVC — Kenhub; cauda = Latin for tail — Dartmouth etymology https://humananatomy.host.dartmouth.edu/BHA/public_html/resources/etymology/Abdominal_viscera.htm
