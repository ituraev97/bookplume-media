# Heart quiz (sheet quiz) — sources
Plate: Gray's Anatomy 1918, Fig. 490 "Front view of heart and lungs" — https://archive.org/details/anatomyofhumanbo00grayrich/page/n527 (public domain); 1918 "Pulmonary artery" = modern pulmonary trunk.
- OpenStax A&P 2e 19.1 Heart Anatomy — https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/19-1-heart-anatomy (SVC drains regions above the diaphragm; RV → pulmonary trunk → pulmonary arteries → lungs; LV muscle much thicker)
- OpenStax A&P 1.6 Anatomical Terminology — https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology/pages/1-6-anatomical-terminology (left/right = the subject's sides)
- Kenhub, Sternocostal surface of the heart — https://www.kenhub.com/en/start/sternocostal-surface-of-the-heart (RV ≈ two-thirds of the front surface)
- Dartmouth Human Anatomy ch. 23 — https://humananatomy.host.dartmouth.edu/BHA/public_html/part_4/chapter_23.html (sternocostal surface mainly RV; conus arteriosus ends in the pulmonary trunk)
- Cleveland Clinic, Pulmonary arteries — https://my.clevelandclinic.org/health/articles/21486-pulmonary-arteries (main pulmonary artery = pulmonary trunk)
- Cleveland Clinic, Aorta — https://my.clevelandclinic.org/health/body/17058-aorta-anatomy (largest blood vessel; arch = curved segment)
- Radiopaedia, Aortic arch — https://radiopaedia.org/articles/aortic-arch
