## e. Facts (checked) and sources
- 3 phases; oral voluntary; pharyngeal + esophageal autonomic; solids 4–8 s, liquids ~1 s; soft palate + uvula close nasopharynx; larynx up, epiglottis down; deglutition apnea; constrictors; UES relaxes; peristalsis circular behind / longitudinal ahead; LES relaxes then keeps acid out; esophagus ~25.4 cm (10 in), thirds skeletal/mixed/smooth, esophageal hiatus; saliva 1–1.5 L/day, 98–99.5% water, salivary amylase; 3 major salivary glands; extrinsic tongue muscles — OpenStax A&P 2e 23.3: https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology/pages/23-3-the-mouth-pharynx-and-esophagus
- Pharyngeal phase starts at the palatoglossal arch, all-or-none reflex, ~1 s; afferents IX/X → solitary tract nucleus; tensor + levator veli palatini; swallowing apnea 0.5–1.5 s; lateral cricoarytenoids + arytenoids close the glottis; suprahyoid elevation; constrictors 20–40 cm/s; UES opened by 3 mechanisms; esophageal peristalsis 3–4 cm/s; innervation table (CN XII tongue except palatoglossus X; mylohyoid V3; digastric V3 + VII; stylopharyngeus IX; constrictors X; cricopharyngeus recurrent laryngeal) — StatPearls, Physiology, Swallowing: https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK541071/ (content read via statpearls.com mirror)
- Pharynx parts, constrictors (cricopharyngeus = part of inferior constrictor), stylopharyngeus = only one on IX, piriform fossa — TeachMeAnatomy: https://teachmeanatomy.info/neck/viscera/pharynx/
- Timing ranges (pharyngeal transit 0.35–1.19 s) — Molfenter & Steele 2012: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3775714/
- Plates: Gray's Anatomy 1918 Figs. 186, 380, 955, 994, 1019, 1032 (rights.json).
