Key takeaways:
• The brain has 4 major regions: cerebrum, diencephalon, brainstem, cerebellum.
• Central sulcus: motor cortex (precentral gyrus) in front, somatosensory cortex (postcentral gyrus) behind.
• The thalamus relays almost all sensory information to the cortex — except smell.
• Brainstem top to bottom = My Pet Mouse: midbrain, pons, medulla. The medulla regulates heart rate, blood pressure and breathing.
• Cranial nerves "2-2-4-4": I–II above the brainstem, III–IV midbrain, V–VIII pons, IX–XII medulla.
