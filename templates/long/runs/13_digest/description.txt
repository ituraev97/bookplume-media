Key takeaways:
• GI tract in order: mouth → pharynx → esophagus → stomach → small intestine → large intestine → anus (Many People Eat Sandwiches Slowly Late Afternoon).
• Stomach: cardia, fundus, body, pylorus; parietal cells make HCl + intrinsic factor, chief cells make pepsinogen.
• Small intestine: duodenum (~10 in), jejunum (~3 ft), ileum (~6 ft, longest) — Dow Jones Industrial.
• Bile and pancreatic juice enter the duodenum at the major duodenal papilla (hepatopancreatic ampulla, sphincter of Oddi).
• Large intestine: cecum, ascending, transverse, descending, sigmoid colon, rectum, anal canal — Could A Tiny Dog Swim Right Away.
