Key takeaways:
• One artery, three name changes: subclavian → axillary at the outer border of the 1st rib, axillary → brachial at the lower border of teres major, brachial → radial + ulnar in the cubital fossa (about the neck of the radius).
• Subclavian branches: VITamin C & D (vertebral, internal thoracic, thyrocervical trunk, costocervical trunk, dorsal scapular); the anterior scalene splits it into 3 parts.
• Axillary branches: Screw The Lawyer, Save A Patient (superior thoracic | thoracoacromial, lateral thoracic | subscapular, anterior + posterior circumflex humeral); pectoralis minor splits it into parts with 1, 2 and 3 branches.
• Brachial artery: runs with the median nerve (lateral → crosses in front → medial); profunda brachii runs with the radial nerve in the radial groove. Cubital fossa lateral → medial: TAN (Tendon of biceps, brachial Artery, median Nerve).
• Radial pulse: at the wrist, just lateral to the flexor carpi radialis tendon. Ulnar artery → superficial palmar arch, radial artery → deep palmar arch (raDial = Deep, sUperficial = Ulnar).
