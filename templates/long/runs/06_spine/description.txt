Key takeaways:
• 7 cervical, 12 thoracic, 5 lumbar ("breakfast at 7, lunch at 12, dinner at 5") + 5 fused sacral vertebrae (sacrum) + usually 4 fused coccygeal (coccyx): 26 bones in an adult.
• 4 curves: thoracic and sacral are primary (present before birth); cervical and lumbar are secondary (head control, then standing and walking).
• A typical vertebra = body + vertebral arch (2 pedicles, 2 laminae) + 7 processes (1 spinous, 2 transverse, 4 articular); the vertebral foramina line up to form the vertebral canal.
• Cervical = transverse foramina (vertebral artery) and bifid spines; C1 atlas has no body and no spinous process ("yes"), C2 axis has the dens ("no"); C7 = vertebra prominens. Thoracic = costal facets for the ribs. Lumbar = big kidney-shaped bodies, no foramina, no rib facets.
• Each intervertebral disc = tough anulus fibrosus around a gel-like nucleus pulposus; key ligaments: anterior and posterior longitudinal, ligamentum flavum, supraspinous (nuchal ligament in the neck).
