Key takeaways:
• 2 compartments: flexors + pronators in front, extensors + supinator in back; flexors start on the medial epicondyle, extensors on the lateral epicondyle.
• Hand trick for the superficial flexors: thumb = pronator teres, index = flexor carpi radialis, middle = palmaris longus, ring = flexor digitorum superficialis, little finger = flexor carpi ulnaris.
• Flexor digitorum profundus is the only muscle that bends the fingertip (DIP) joint; the superficialis tendon splits so the profundus tendon can pass through.
• Back of the forearm counts 1-2-2-1 (brachioradialis, 2 radial wrist extensors, 2 finger extensors, extensor carpi ulnaris); deep layer = supinator, 3 thumb muscles, extensor indicis. Anatomical snuffbox: APL + EPB on one side, EPL on the other.
• Nerves: back = radial; front = median, except flexor carpi ulnaris and the ulnar half of flexor digitorum profundus (ulnar nerve).
