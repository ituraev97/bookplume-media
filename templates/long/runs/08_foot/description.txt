Key takeaways:
• 26 bones in each foot: 7 tarsals + 5 metatarsals + 14 phalanges. Hindfoot = talus + calcaneus; midfoot = navicular, cuboid, 3 cuneiforms; forefoot = metatarsals + phalanges.
• Talus: the highest bone of the foot, sits between the tibia and fibula, carries the body's weight into the foot — and no muscles attach to it. Calcaneus: the heel bone, the largest bone of the foot; the Achilles tendon attaches to the calcaneal tuberosity.
• Navicular (tuberosity → tibialis posterior), cuboid (groove for fibularis longus), medial / intermediate / lateral cuneiforms (medial cuneiform → tibialis anterior). Base of the 5th metatarsal → fibularis brevis. The big toe (hallux) has 2 phalanges, toes 2–5 have 3.
• Ankle (talocrural) joint = synovial hinge between tibia, fibula and talus; medial malleolus = tibia, lateral malleolus = fibula; most stable in dorsiflexion because the talus is wider in front. Deltoid ligament medially; ATFL, CFL, PTFL laterally — the ATFL is the weakest and most often injured.
• Subtalar joint (talus + calcaneus) = inversion and eversion. Three arches — medial longitudinal (highest), lateral longitudinal (flatter), transverse — supported by ligaments, muscles and the plantar aponeurosis.
