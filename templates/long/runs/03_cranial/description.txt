Key takeaways:
• 12 pairs attach directly to the brain and are numbered I–XII from front to back; I and II attach to the forebrain, III–XII to the brainstem.
• Names in order — Ooh Ooh Ooh To Touch And Feel Very Good Velvet. Such Heaven!
• Sensory, motor or both — Some Say Marry Money, But My Brother Says Big Brains Matter More (sensory: I, II, VIII · motor: III, IV, VI, XI, XII · both: V, VII, IX, X).
• Eye muscles — LR6 SO4 R3: lateral rectus = abducens (VI), superior oblique = trochlear (IV), the rest = oculomotor (III).
• Classic exam trap: feeling in the face = trigeminal (V, with divisions V1, V2, V3); moving the face = facial (VII).
