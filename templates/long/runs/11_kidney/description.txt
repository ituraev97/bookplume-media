Key takeaways:
• The kidneys are retroperitoneal, from about T12 to L3; the right kidney sits a little lower because of the liver.
• 4 layers from inside out: renal capsule, perirenal fat, renal (Gerota's) fascia, pararenal fat.
• At the hilum, front to back: renal Vein, renal Artery, renal Pelvis (V-A-P).
• Urine path: renal papilla → minor calyx → major calyx → renal pelvis → ureter.
• Arteries in order: renal → segmental → interlobar → arcuate → cortical radiate → afferent arteriole → glomerulus ("Really Smart Interns Always Check Answers").
• Nephron in order: Bowman's capsule → proximal convoluted tubule → loop of Henle → distal convoluted tubule → collecting duct ("Big People Love Doing Crosswords").
