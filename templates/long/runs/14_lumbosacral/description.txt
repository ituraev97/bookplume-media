Key takeaways:
• Lumbar plexus = anterior rami of L1–L4 (+ T12), formed inside psoas major; 6 branches in order: I, I Get Leftovers On Fridays (iliohypogastric, ilioinguinal, genitofemoral, lateral femoral cutaneous, obturator, femoral).
• Femoral nerve (L2–L4) = front of the thigh (quadriceps, knee extension); obturator nerve (L2–L4) = adductors of the inner thigh.
• Sacral plexus = L4–S4 on the piriformis; only the superior gluteal nerve leaves ABOVE piriformis. Some Irish Sailor Pesters Polly.
• Sciatic nerve (L4–S3), the largest nerve in the body, splits into tibial and common fibular nerves above the knee.
• PED and TIP: Peroneal Everts and Dorsiflexes, Tibial Inverts and Plantarflexes.
