Key takeaways:
• The brachial plexus is the nerve network of the upper limb, built from the ventral rami of C5, C6, C7, C8 and T1.
• 5 parts in order — Really Tired? Drink Cold Brew: Roots, Trunks, Divisions, Cords, Branches (5 · 3 · 6 · 3 · 5).
• Trunks: C5+C6 superior, C7 middle, C8+T1 inferior. Each trunk splits into anterior and posterior divisions beneath the clavicle.
• Cords are named by their position around the axillary artery: lateral (C5–C7), medial (C8–T1), posterior (C5–T1).
• Terminal branches: musculocutaneous (elbow flexion), axillary (deltoid, teres minor), radial (extensors), median (forearm flexors, thenar muscles), ulnar (most small hand muscles; the "funny bone").
