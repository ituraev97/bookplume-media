Key takeaways:
• Conducting zone (nose → terminal bronchioles) moves, filters, warms and humidifies air; the respiratory zone (respiratory bronchioles → alveoli) exchanges gas.
• Pharynx in 3 parts: nasopharynx (air only), oropharynx (air + food), laryngopharynx (→ larynx in front, esophagus behind).
• Larynx: 9 cartilages — thyroid (Adam's apple), cricoid (a full ring, wide at the back), epiglottis, plus paired arytenoid, corniculate and cuneiform.
• Trachea: 16–20 C-shaped rings of hyaline cartilage, open at the back (trachealis); it splits at the carina, and the right main bronchus is wider, shorter and more vertical.
• Right lung: 3 lobes, horizontal + oblique fissures. Left lung: 2 lobes, oblique fissure, cardiac notch and lingula. Visceral and parietal pleura with the pleural cavity between.
• Type I alveolar cells = thin, for gas exchange; type II = surfactant. The diaphragm (phrenic nerve, C3–C5: "C3, 4, 5 keeps the diaphragm alive") does most of quiet breathing.
