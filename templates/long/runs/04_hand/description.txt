Key takeaways:
• 27 bones per hand: 8 carpals + 5 metacarpals + 14 phalanges.
• Carpal bones, thumb side first — proximal row: scaphoid, lunate, triquetrum, pisiform; distal row: trapezium, trapezoid, capitate, hamate. Mnemonic: Sally Left The Party To Take Cathy Home.
• Scaphoid = most commonly fractured carpal bone; capitate = largest; trapezoid = smallest; hamate has a hook; pisiform is a sesamoid bone.
• Trapezium vs trapezoid: trapeziUM sits by the thUMb.
• Metacarpals are numbered 1–5 from the thumb; the thumb has 2 phalanges, every other finger has 3. Joints are named after the bones they join.
