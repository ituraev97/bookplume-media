- Radiopaedia, Branches of ophthalmic artery (mnemonic): https://radiopaedia.org/articles/branches-of-ophthalmic-artery-mnemonic — "Smart Little Pipes Supply All My Sight's Demands" = short posterior ciliary, long posterior ciliary, posterior ethmoidal, supraorbital, anterior ethmoidal, medial palpebral, supratrochlear, dorsal nasal (page notes the order is not the true branching order; lacrimal + central retinal are separate early branches).
- Kenhub, Ophthalmic artery: https://www.kenhub.com/en/library/anatomy/ophthalmic-artery — posterior ciliary supply sclera, choroid, anterior eye; long posterior ciliary join the major arterial circle of the iris; posterior ethmoidal → posterior ethmoidal cells + nasal septum; supraorbital → forehead skin, muscles, periosteum; anterior ethmoidal → ethmoidal cells, lateral nasal wall, septum; medial palpebral → upper + lower eyelid; terminal branches = supratrochlear (forehead skin) + dorsal nasal (dorsum of nose, nasolacrimal sac); Kenhub lists 10 branches incl. central retinal, muscular, lacrimal (not in the mnemonic).
