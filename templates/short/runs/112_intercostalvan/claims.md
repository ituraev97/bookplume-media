# Claims to verify (intercostal VAN mnemonic short)
1. In each intercostal space (ribs 1-11) a neurovascular bundle of intercostal VEIN, ARTERY and NERVE runs along the lower (inferior) border of the rib above.
2. Order from superior to inferior = vein, artery, nerve -> mnemonic "VAN".
3. The bundle sits in the COSTAL GROOVE on the inner surface / inferior border of the rib (Gray 1918 text for Fig. 122: "Between it [internal surface ridge] and the inferior border is a groove, the costal groove, for the intercostal vessels and nerve").
4. (Own diagram) the bundle runs between the internal intercostal and innermost intercostal muscle layers - NOT spoken, only if shown.
5. Quick test answer: the NERVE is the lowest of the three.
6. Plate = Gray's Anatomy 1918 Fig. 819 "Diagram of the course and branches of a typical intercostal nerve" (public domain).
