# Claims to verify (wrist extensor compartments mnemonic short)
Compartments radial -> ulnar, tendon count 2-2-1-2-1-1 (total 9):
1 abductor pollicis longus (APL) + extensor pollicis brevis (EPB)  [thumb pair; lateral border of anatomical snuffbox]
2 extensor carpi radialis longus (ECRL) + brevis (ECRB)  [extend/abduct wrist; insert 2nd/3rd metacarpal bases]
3 extensor pollicis longus (EPL)  [one tendon; Lister's tubercle sits between compartments 2 and 3]
4 extensor digitorum (ED) + extensor indicis (EI)  [fingers]
5 extensor digiti minimi (EDM)  [little finger; digiti minimi = of the little finger]
6 extensor carpi ulnaris (ECU)  [last, ulnar side, groove beside ulnar head]
Mnemonic "2-2-1-2-1-1": source Epomedicine (https://epomedicine.com/medical-students/wrist-extensor-compartments).
Quick test answer: extensor of just the little finger = extensor digiti minimi = compartment 5.
1918 Gray Fig. 1234 labels: "Ext. digiti quinti prop." (EDM), "Extensor digitorum communis", "Ext. indicis propius", "Ext. pollicis longus/brevis", "Abd. poll. long.", "Ext. carpi rad. long./brev.", "Ext. carpi ulnaris".
Plate = Gray's Anatomy 1918 Fig. 1234 "The mucous sheaths of the tendons on the back of the wrist" (left hand, dorsal view, thumb on the right).
Own diagram claims: ECRL/ECRB end at 2nd/3rd metacarpal bases; ECU at 5th metacarpal base; APL at 1st metacarpal base; EPB at thumb proximal phalanx base; EPL at thumb distal phalanx; ED to digits 2-5 (EI joins index); EDM to little finger.
