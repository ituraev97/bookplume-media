# Sources — epidermis layers mnemonic short (2026-11-27, 14:31 run)
- StatPearls, Anatomy, Skin (Integument), Epidermis: https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK470464/ — five strata; lucidum thin, clear, thick skin (palms, soles); spinosum desmosomes ("prickle cell layer"); basale stem cells, also called germinativum.
- UEN Pressbooks A&P, Epidermis: https://uen.pressbooks.pub/anatomyphysiology/chapter/epidermis/ — thick skin 5 layers, thin skin 4 (no lucidum); corneum dead keratinized cells; basale stem-cell mitosis.
- Mnemonic "Come, Let's Get Sun Burned" (C-L-G-S-B): student-circulated (Brainscape deck), not textbook-canonical → spoken as "A popular mnemonic".
- Gray's Anatomy 1918 (archive.org anatomyofhumanbo00grayrich) Fig. 941 "Section of epidermis (Ranvier)": printed labels corneum, lucidum, granulosum, mucosum, germinativum (cropped off; modern names overlaid).
